Prisma Studio is now licensed under the plain, unmodified Apache License 2.0. The previous extra requirement to keep Prisma branding visible is gone: you can embed Studio and remove or replace its Prisma branding without a commercial license. The Apache 2.0 license grants no trademark rights, so rebranded builds must not be presented as official Prisma products.
