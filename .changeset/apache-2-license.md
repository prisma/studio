Prisma Studio is now licensed under the plain, unmodified Apache License 2.0. The previous extra requirement to keep Prisma branding visible is gone: you can embed Studio and remove or replace its Prisma branding without a commercial license. This also applies to every previously published release of `@prisma/studio-core`, regardless of the license it originally stated. The Apache 2.0 license grants no rights to the Prisma name or logos beyond reasonable and customary use to describe the work's origin and reproduce the NOTICE content (section 6), so rebranded builds must not be presented as official Prisma products.
